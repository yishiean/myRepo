Key moments 流れ

Coming home

  1. Press power.
  2. The TV opens on Home Hub, not LG's home.
  3. You see the evening scene and today's note.

Leaving a note

  1. Open Home Hub on your phone (saved to your home screen).
  2. Note tab, type, Send.
  3. It appears on the TV within about a second.

Movie night

  1. Tap Movie night on the phone.
  2. The room dims and the TV switches to the ink-wash scene.
  3. Press OK on Netflix, or the phone opens it for you (phase 2).

What to know up front 注意

Limit

It sits on top of LG's home, it doesn't replace it. Without rooting, Home Hub is an app. We make it feel like home by having it open first. Turning on Quick Start+ and turning off Home Auto Launch should make the TV resume Home Hub when you power it on. I need to test that on webOS 26.

Phase 1

Runs in the TV's web browser. Everything works, and you can be using it tonight. The app dock highlights the app, and you open it with the remote buttons (Netflix and Disney+ have their own buttons).

Phase 2

Installed as a real TV app through LG Developer Mode. No browser bar, and the dock and Movie night can open Netflix, YouTube and Disney+ directly. The trade-off: you tap Extend in the Developer Mode app every few weeks, or the app stops loading.

Check

OLED care. Static elements like the clock and dock drift a few pixels every minute, and the screen dims after 20 minutes idle. Pixel Refresher keeps doing its job as usual.
